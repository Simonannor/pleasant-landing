# Springs Chapel

'Pleasant Springs Chapel is a vibrant Christian community located at Achimota Mile 7 in Accra, Ghana, operating on the premises of Kaspong Ltd.. Dedicated to their mission of "Pleasing God, Serving Our Generation," the chapel hosts its primary Sunday Service at 8:30 AM. It also serves as the current home for The Father's Heart School of Ministry. [1, 2]
